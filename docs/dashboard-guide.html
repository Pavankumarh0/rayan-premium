The things you will actually do

Answer an enquiry

  1. Open Enquiries. New ones are at the top.
  2. Click the person’s name to see their trip details and message.
  3. Use the Call, WhatsApp or Email buttons on the right.
  4. Set the Status — New, In progress, Quoted, Closed or Spam — and add a note for your own records. Notes are never shown to the customer.
Nobody is emailed when an enquiry arrives. SMTP has not been set up, so requests sit in the dashboard until someone opens it. Check it daily, or ask your developer to add email alerts in Settings.

Change wording on a page

  1. Open Pages & sections and click the page.
  2. You will see its blocks in the order they appear, each showing a line of its own wording so you can tell them apart.
  3. Click Edit on the block you want, change the words, save.
  4. Use the arrows to reorder, the eye to hide a block without deleting it, or Add a section for something new.

Change a price

  1. Open Tariffs and click the table.
  2. Scroll to Rates. The columns on screen are the columns on the website.
  3. Edit any cell, tick Highlight to emphasise a row, then save.

Add a photograph

  1. Open Media and upload the image.
  2. Click Copy address.
  3. Go wherever you want it — a vehicle, a service, the gallery — and paste it into the image field.
  4. Write the alt text: a plain description of what is in the photograph.

Change the phone number, email or address

Settings → Contact details. One change updates the header, the footer, the contact page, every call button and the business details search engines read. Do not retype a number anywhere else.

Why something is not showing up

The site deliberately hides anything incomplete rather than publishing it half-finished. If something will not appear, it is almost always one of these.

What you expectedWhy it is hidden
A vehicle, service, guide or route Its Status is Draft. Set it to Published.
A testimonial It needs both Published and Verified. Verified means a real, named customer actually said it.
A destination’s distance or journey time Facts verified is off. The figures stay hidden until somebody confirms them.
Prices anywhere Two switches: Settings → Display → Show prices, and the individual item’s own price switch.
Call or WhatsApp buttons The number is empty in Settings → Contact details. The buttons hide rather than showing a dead link.
A page section Its eye icon is off, or the page itself is Draft.
The whole site, in Google Check the SEO page. It will tell you exactly what is blocking it.

Four things worth being careful with

Changing a URL slug

The slug is the part of the address after the slash. Changing it breaks every existing link — anything printed, shared on WhatsApp, or indexed by Google. If you must change one, add a rule under Redirects from the old address to the new one.

Deleting

Deleting is permanent. Almost every time, what you actually want is to set the status to Draft, or hide the section — both keep the wording and the address, and both can be undone in a click.

Alt text

Every image asks for a description. It is read aloud to people using screen readers, shown if the image fails to load, and read by search engines. “Image” or “photo” helps nobody — describe what is actually in the picture.

The last administrator

The dashboard will not let you remove the only administrator, or remove your own administrator access. Keep at least two administrator accounts, so losing one does not lock everyone out.

These are not dashboard problems — they need a decision or an action from your side.
